About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a specialized university in Malaysia focusing on marine science, aquaculture, and aquatic resource management. It has grown from a small marine science center into a premier university dedicated to safeguarding and harnessing the potential of tropical marine resources.

The campus is located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, providing a conducive environment for research, learning, and coastal community engagement.
